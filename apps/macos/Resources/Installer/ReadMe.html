CONNECT FOR MAC

Before you install

Save your work and quit Connect (⌘Q) before continuing. Closing its window keeps the app running.

Connect will be installed in /Applications/Connect.app. If an older version is there, this installer replaces the app while keeping your account data and preferences.

Requires macOS 13 or later and an internet connection. macOS may ask you to authorize installation into Applications.
