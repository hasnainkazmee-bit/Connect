COLABB / IDENTITY AND WORK RECORD MARKS

Reusable vector devices from the proposed profile reputation study.
White first; orange #FF6A00; ink #172C38. Use with a clear text label.

ON COLABB
A company-managed account on Colabb. This is an identity/participation mark, not a verified-license seal. Display beside the company name; never use for unclaimed public listings.

FIRST JOB COMPLETED
One distinct job accepted as finished by its hiring company. A selected proposal or an approved task does not count as a completed job.

TEN JOBS COMPLETED
Ten distinct jobs accepted as finished by their hiring companies. Count each finished job once. Exclude cancelled or incomplete work.

FIVE RETURNING HIRERS
Five distinct hiring companies have each completed at least two jobs with this provider. More work with the same hirer does not increase the distinct-hirer count.

The work milestones are proposed features. Their sample counts do not describe real companies. None certify quality, verify credentials, or guarantee future performance.

Keep marks at 24 px or larger. Use each SVG at its original square ratio. For an unearned milestone, use the monochrome/muted treatment and a visible progress count; never present it as earned. Do not put text inside the marks.
