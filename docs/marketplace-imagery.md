## Hero prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original editorial photograph for Connect by Sunhub, a premium B2B solar hiring marketplace landing page. Create a wide 3:2 landscape photograph, approximately 2400 pixels wide, no text or logos. Scene: a real-feeling commercial solar installation on a pale concrete flat roof. A precise high oblique overhead view, looking down at about 65 degrees, of two small groups of working technicians in white helmets and muted orange safety shirts positioned along an unfinished row of deep blue-black solar modules. The human scale is essential: three or four people occupy a small part of the scene, actually working together beside safely laid out aluminum mounting rails and a flat panel, not posing. Framing: striking architectural geometry, long regular rows of existing panels on the right two-thirds, an open pale gray roof walkway and low pallet of panels on the left third, strong diagonal composition, crop close enough to see the people. Natural late-afternoon directional sunlight, crisp long shadows, restrained colors (ink blue, chalk gray, small safety orange accents), tactile realistic panel glass and galvanized metal, subtle film grain. Authentic commercial architecture photography, discerning editorial photo book, photographed on a medium-format camera, controlled highlights and no exaggerated HDR. This is a calm, exceptionally well-composed documentary-style image about skilled work, not a glossy solar stock advertising photo. Keep the central 70 percent safe for a wide 2.6:1 website crop. No horizon, no sunset, no wind turbines, no lens flare, no glowing graphics, no collage, no floating interface, no words, no logos or watermark. Workers have plausible proportions, work safely on a broad flat roof far from its edges, gloves and helmets.

## Planning prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original premium editorial image for Connect by Sunhub's solar company hiring marketplace. A portrait 4:5 photograph of the quiet planning work behind solar installations. Tight, oblique overhead crop of a large pale gray workbench in a small solar engineering workshop. A pair of naturally proportioned hands in navy work-shirt sleeves compare a folded solar rooftop layout drawing with a physical small black solar cell sample and a metal ruler. The drawing contains fine architectural lines and module rectangles but no readable lettering. A simple orange carpenter's pencil and one rolled drawing at the edge. Subjects are the skilled hands, the tactile technical paper and the beautiful light; no face. Scene deliberately spare and credible, not a styled clutter of props. Natural directional window light, soft crisp shadows, ink navy, chalk white, brushed aluminum and one small vivid orange detail. Photograph like a thoughtful architecture magazine commission, subtle grain, restrained color, rich but not crushed shadows, genuine paper and hand texture. Diagonal composition, precise crop, no fake dashboard, no labels, no words, no logos, no watermark, no glowing graphics, no dramatic blue lighting. Keep all fingers natural and tools physically plausible. This is a companion to an overhead commercial solar installation image.
